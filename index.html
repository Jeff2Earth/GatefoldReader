00Input

Used only for single-channel raw FITS/TIFF subs. ZWO color cameras (ASI585MC, ASI533MC, ASI2600MC) are RGGB. If colors come out magenta or swapped, try the others.

01Background neutralization

Measures the sky level in each channel and evens them out, which removes the green or brown tint in empty sky.

02Channel balance

“From stars” assumes the average star is white, which keeps the nebula’s real red. “Gray world” forces the whole frame neutral and can mute Hα.

03Green removal (SCNR)

SCNR caps green at the average of red and blue (Average) or at the larger of the two (Maximum). On its own, that turns teal OIII into plain blue. Teal protection spares pixels where blue nearly matches green, so a teal core keeps its color while green sky and stars are still cleaned. Teal boost adds saturation to cyan and teal only.

04Stretch

A linked midtones stretch, so it brightens without shifting color.

05Saturation

Why your nebulae come out green

Half the pixels in an RGGB Bayer matrix are green, and the IMX585 sensor is most sensitive in green. Light-pollution and multi-band filters like the RGB Ultra II also pass a strong green band, where skyglow and OIII both land. The stack ends up with a lifted, noisy green channel even after debayering.

Recommended order

ASIAIR JPG workflow: open the stacked, stretched JPG. The tool detects it as already stretched, then background neutralization and Auto from stars make the first pass. Raise SCNR until the stars and sky look neutral, and adjust the black point to taste. For targets with real OIII (Veil, Ring, Dumbbell), switch to Maximum neutral so the teal survives.

JPG is 8-bit and the color is already baked in by the stretch, so heavy corrections can show banding. If you want more headroom later, save the stack as a FITS file as well and open that here instead.

File support

FITS (8, 16, 32-bit int or float; RGB cubes or raw Bayer), TIFF (8, 16, 32-bit, including float), PNG and JPEG. Browsers read PNG as 8-bit, so use FITS or TIFF to keep the full dynamic range. Your files never leave this computer.
