# Welcome to Gatefold Reader

Gatefold Reader transforms your phone, foldable device, tablet or computer into an animated digital book. It combines the convenience of an e-reader with realistic pages, an authentic center binding and natural page-turning gestures.

Your books remain on your device. Gatefold Reader does not upload your personal books to a server.

## Install Gatefold Reader

### Android

1. Open Gatefold Reader in Chrome.
2. Tap the browser’s three-dot menu.
3. Select **Install App** or **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Confirm the installation.
5. Launch Gatefold Reader from its new app icon.

### iPhone or iPad

1. Open Gatefold Reader in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Select **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Turn on **Open as Web App**, if shown.
5. Tap **Add**.

Gatefold Reader will now open in its own app-style window.

## Find Books to Read

Gatefold Reader opens EPUB ebooks and also supports compatible TXT, DOCX and PDF documents.

Public-domain ebooks can be downloaded legally from:

* [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/) — Thousands of free public-domain books in EPUB and other formats.
* [Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org/) — Carefully formatted and professionally typeset public-domain EPUB books.
* [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/) — Search, discover and borrow millions of books.
* [Internet Archive Books and Texts](https://archive.org/details/texts) — A large collection of downloadable and borrowable historical books and documents.

On a book website, select the **EPUB** download whenever it is available. The downloaded book will normally appear in your device’s **Downloads** folder or Files application.

Books protected by digital-rights management, sometimes called DRM, may not open outside the store or application from which they were purchased. Some borrowed books from Open Library or Internet Archive may therefore require their designated reading application.

## Load a Book

1. Open the Gatefold Reader toolbar.
2. Tap **Load Book**.
3. Locate the book in Downloads, Files, Google Drive or another available storage location.
4. Select the book.
5. Allow a moment for Gatefold Reader to prepare and paginate it.

Large illustrated books and study Bibles can take longer to load because they contain significantly more text, images and internal sections.

Gatefold Reader remembers your most recently opened book and reading position on that device.

## Open the Toolbar

Tap near the upper center of the reading screen to reveal the toolbar. The toolbar closes automatically so it does not remain in the way while you read.

The active area extends slightly below the buttons, making the toolbar easier to open without interfering with page turns near the upper corners.

## Turn Pages

Drag a page from an outer corner or edge as though you were turning a physical sheet of paper.

You can:

* Drag forward to turn to the next page.
* Reverse direction before releasing to return the page.
* Tap near an outside edge for a quick page turn.
* Use the Previous and Next controls when available.
* Use the left and right arrow keys on a physical keyboard.

The page follows your movement, allowing you to inspect the next page before deciding whether to complete the turn.

## Choose the Page Layout

The layout control offers three modes:

* **Auto:** Portrait orientation displays one page. Landscape orientation displays an open two-page spread.
* **Fold:** Forces a single-page view.
* **Unfold:** Forces a two-page book spread.

Auto mode is recommended for most readers. On a folding phone, Gatefold Reader responds to both screen posture and orientation while preserving your reading position.

## Use the Index

Tap **Index** to view the chapters or sections detected inside the current book.

Selecting an entry takes you directly to that location. Gatefold Reader organizes recognized chapters in reading order and begins major chapters on a fresh page whenever the book structure permits.

Complex or unusually formatted ebooks may provide limited index information.

## Search Bible References

When a recognized Bible is open, use the reference box to enter a passage such as:

**John 3:16**

Press **Enter** on the keyboard to move to the requested passage. The Bible index also allows you to select a book, chapter and verse.

Very large or highly illustrated Bible editions may require additional preparation time before their complete index becomes available.

## Add a Bookmark

Tap **Bookmark** to save your current location. Return to the bookmark control to revisit saved positions.

Reading progress and bookmarks are stored locally on the device and browser where they were created. Clearing browser data, uninstalling the PWA or using a private/incognito tab may remove locally stored information.

## Change the Appearance

Open **Reader Settings** to adjust:

* Text size
* Font
* Line spacing
* Page margins
* Page layout
* Page-turn sound
* Fullscreen display
* Reading theme

The Theme button cycles through:

1. X-Light
2. Light
3. Papyrus
4. Ancient
5. Dark

**Papyrus** provides a brighter, softly aged page. **Ancient** uses deeper edge shading and a more weathered appearance. Theme changes do not alter the contents of the book.

## Read Offline

After Gatefold Reader has been installed and its required files have been cached, the reader can operate without an internet connection.

Books already stored on your device can be loaded locally. A first visit, an application update or an externally hosted resource may require internet access.

For dependable offline use, open Gatefold Reader once while connected and allow the app to finish loading before going offline.

## Switching Devices

Books, bookmarks and reading positions are not automatically transferred between devices. Load the book separately on each device.

Because pagination depends on screen size, orientation, font and margins, the displayed page number may differ between a phone, foldable device and tablet even when the same text is being shown.

## Troubleshooting

If a new version does not appear immediately:

1. Close Gatefold Reader completely.
2. Reopen it while connected to the internet.
3. Refresh the browser page if necessary.
4. Allow the service worker and cached files time to update.

If a book does not open, confirm that it is an unprotected EPUB, TXT, DOCX or PDF file. Extremely large or complex ebooks may need additional processing time.
